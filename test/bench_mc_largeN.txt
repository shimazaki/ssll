Reading: mc consistently recovers the pairwise structure better than
pseudo+mf (th2 error and corr, eta rmse at both N). The poor mc first-order
numbers at N=60 are a fixed-budget artifact: the mc mll trace is still
descending steeply at iteration 10 (slow first-order components not yet
converged), whereas pseudo+mf plateaus by ~iteration 6. See the 30-iteration
N=60 run below.
